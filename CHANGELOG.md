# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release for Minecraft Bedrock, with the same recipes as Ore Cutter 0.1.0 for Java.

- Craft any deepslate ore from a block of its material and one deepslate: block of coal, copper,
  iron, gold, redstone, emerald, lapis or diamond. For copper, iron and gold, the smelted block
  and the raw block both work.
- Coal, copper, iron, gold, redstone, emerald, lapis and diamond ore each cut to their deepslate
  twin in the stonecutter, and each deepslate ore cuts back. One block in, one block out.
